# Code of Conduct

Krabka contributors must keep project spaces safe, respectful, and useful.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code without attacking people.
- Give specific, actionable feedback.
- Respect different backgrounds, identities, experiences, and levels of expertise.
- Stop behavior when a maintainer says that it harms another participant or the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not accepted in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the contact route in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may edit or remove content and may warn, suspend, or ban participants when needed to protect the community.

Enforcement decisions will consider context, severity, repetition, and risk to affected people.
